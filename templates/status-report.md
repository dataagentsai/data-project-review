## What good looks like

Three plain lines anyone can read in a minute: what we learned, what changed, and what we need from you. The colour follows a written rule, not a feeling, so amber appears early with a recovery step instead of green turning straight to red. Bad news is reported within days of being found, with its impact and a next step. The forecast comes from the real rate of progress, not the original plan. The assumptions the plan depends on are repeated every time, so nobody is surprised when one breaks.
