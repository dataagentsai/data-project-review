# Stakeholder map and communication plan: [project name]

Last updated: [date] by [name]

## What good looks like

The map names everyone who can approve, use or block the project, not just the sponsor and the people in the weekly meeting. Source system owners, operations, security, the platform team and downstream users are on it from discovery, because meeting them at go-live is too late. Each kind of decision has exactly one named decider. For each stakeholder there is a line on what success means to them and when the team last spoke to them. A contact and escalation list says who to call when something breaks, with a deputy for each.
