## What good looks like

A short, live log, not a list of worries. Each top risk has one named person as owner, a trigger that tells you it is happening, a mitigation that is actually being done with a due date, and a recent review date. The riskiest assumptions are tested, not just listed, and every assumption in the estimate also appears here. Dependencies have an owner outside the team and a date they committed to. Issues are raised within days, and the status report never mentions a problem that is not in this log.
