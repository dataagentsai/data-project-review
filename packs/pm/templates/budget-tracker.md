## What good looks like

Every line of the budget points to something written down: the estimate's middle figure and its assumptions, day rates, quotes for licences and environments, and the running cost measured from a real run. Spend is compared with plan every month against the share of work finished, so an overspend shows months early, and the forecast at completion moves when the facts move. Contingency is held as one visible amount, sized to the top risks, released only by a named person, and every drawdown says what it paid for. Costs that land in other teams' budgets (source changes, vendor fees, the old system during a parallel run) are listed with who pays. Each approved change adds its build and running cost to the forecast. The running cost after go-live has an owner who has accepted it, for this year and next.
