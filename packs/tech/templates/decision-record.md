# Decision records: [project name]

## What good looks like

Every decision that someone could later reopen is written down within a day: the question, the options considered, the choice, the reason, who decided, who signed and when. The consequences are stated in business words, so the person signing understands what changes for them. Open decisions have an owner, a deadline and a default that applies if nobody decides. The log is kept current to the end of the project and handed over, so the support team can answer "why does it work like this?" without calling the old team.
